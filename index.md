# Seamrise privacy policy

**Effective date:** November 23, 2026

Seamrise is an Android app that stitches overlapping photos into a panorama. It is made by Informaticuche.
This page explains what the app does with your information.

## In short

Seamrise does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no adverts,
no analytics and no crash reporting, and it does not ask for any permission, including access to the internet.
Your photos are processed on your phone and never leave it unless you choose to share them.

## Your photos

- You choose the photos with Android's photo picker. The app sees only the photos you pick, not the rest of your
  gallery.
- While you work, the app keeps copies of the picked photos and small thumbnails of them in its own private storage
  on your phone, and the panorama in its memory. It replaces the copies each time you pick new photos, and Android
  can delete them at any time to free space. Other apps cannot read them.
- **Save** puts the panorama in your phone's `Pictures/Seamrise` folder, where your gallery app shows it. It stays
  there until you delete it, also after the app is uninstalled.
- **Share** hands the panorama to the app you pick in Android's share menu. What happens to it then depends on that
  app and its own privacy policy. The app keeps the shared copy in its private storage until the next time you open
  the app.

## Your settings

The app remembers its settings (the resolution, the crop page choice and the theme) on your phone. If Android's
backup is turned on, Android may include these settings in your device's backup with Google, under your Google
account; the app's photos and panoramas are not part of it.

## What the app does not do

- It does not connect to the internet: Android does not let it, as it does not ask for that permission.
- It does not read your location, contacts, camera, microphone or any identifier of your phone, and it has no access
  to your advertising ID.
- It does not sell or share any information with anyone.

## Children

The app is not directed at children, and it collects no information from anyone, children included.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it keeps in its private storage. To remove saved panoramas, delete them in
your gallery app or the `Pictures/Seamrise` folder. You can also clear the app's storage at any time in Android's
Settings > Apps > Seamrise > Storage.

## Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated before that version of the app is
released, and the effective date above will change.
